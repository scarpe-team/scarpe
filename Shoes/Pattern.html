Overview

What a background or a border paints with: a colour, a gradient or an image (manual 785-790, 2754-2755, 2807-2809, ledger D9). Backgrounds, borders and gradients are patterns, and any of them can paint another background or border:

stripe = background tomato border stripe.to_pattern, strokewidth: 2
